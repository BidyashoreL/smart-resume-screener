Job Title: Data Scientist

We are looking for a Data Scientist to help drive data-informed product
decisions.

Required Skills:
- Python
- Machine Learning
- SQL

Preferred Skills:
- scikit-learn
- AWS
- Data Visualization

Minimum Experience: 2 years

Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Statistics, Computer Science,
Mathematics, or a related quantitative field.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets to identify actionable business insights
- Build and evaluate predictive models for churn, pricing, and forecasting
- Communicate findings clearly to non-technical stakeholders
- Partner with engineering to productionize models where appropriate
